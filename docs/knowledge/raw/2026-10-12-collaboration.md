# HumTrack research: collaboration, community and creator-marketplace platforms (as of 2026-10-12)

Every claim below comes from a source I fetched or searched. Anything marked **[unverified]** comes from a single weak source (a promotional blog, an aggregator) or is my own inference.

## 1. Platform-by-platform findings

### BandLab: the giant this space has to beat
- **Scale.** BandLab passed 100M registered users in March 2024, up from 60M in January 2023. The US is about 30% of users. A Series B1 in May 2023 valued it at $425M post-money. It launched distribution with FUGA in November 2023. [MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/music-making-app-bandlab-surpasses-100-million-users)
- **Model.** Creation is free. Revenue comes from Membership (which includes distribution) and from paid promotion: "Boost" and "Promote" ad campaigns. The CEO says Gen Z is the core user base and that "music creation is becoming a social activity". AI-tool users grew 15x in 2023. [MBW, Dec 2023](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/bandlabs-meng-ru-kuok-entrepreneur-year-mbw-goldman/)
- **Tiers.** There are now Pro and Max tiers. Members get weekly quotas for royalty-free sample and beat "claims". "Credits" pay for Boost, and "Tokens" pay for generative AI such as SongStarter. [BandLab Help](https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/55199825610777-Credits-and-Claims)
- **Membership perks.** Membership includes Opportunities (label, festival and mentorship placements), Distribution with full ownership kept, and beta tools including Audio-to-MIDI. [BandLab Help](https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/19290698933145-What-is-BandLab-Membership)
- **Fan Reach.** This is an email/fan-list tool. Free users get limited use; members get unlimited campaigns and fans. [BandLab Help](https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/22139485730201-What-is-Fan-Reach)
- **How collaboration works.** Projects live in the cloud and collaborators invited to a project work mostly asynchronously. The creator can turn on "forking", which gives someone an independent copy. BandLab tracks who added what but has **no built-in credit-split tool and does not arbitrate ownership**. [Audeobox, Feb 2026](https://www.audeobox.com/learn/bandlab/bandlab-collaboration-features/)
- **AI licensing.** In July 2025 BandLab added a per-song "Open to AI licensing" opt-in. Each AI training deal needs explicit approval from the rights holder. [BandLab Technologies](https://bandlabtechnologies.com/news/bandlab-licensing-platform-artists-rights-holders-ai-companies-legal-ai-music-models/)
- **Weakness.** One 2023 analysis argues BandLab built creators (supply) faster than listeners (demand). d4vd's "Romantic Homicide" charted on the Billboard Hot 100, yet he had only about 8k followers and 19k plays on BandLab; successful artists move their audiences to TikTok and YouTube. [Alderbrook](https://alderbrook.substack.com/p/bandlab-can-the-creation-platform)

### Collaboration-first platforms that died or shrank
- **Endlesss** (real-time jam app, launched 2016–2019) shut down on 31 May 2024. No reason was stated. [CDM](https://cdm.link/2024/05/endlesss-discontinued/), [Sonicstate](https://sonicstate.com/news/2024/05/25/endlesss-is-closing-down-sad-news)
  - Wikipedia says the app later passed to Imogen Heap's HabLab. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Exile)
  - **Blend.io** (acquired by ROLI) closed at the same time; users had until 4 June 2024 to sync their projects. [CDM](https://cdm.link/2024/05/endlesss-discontinued/)
- **Splice Studio** (collaboration and version control) closed in 2023.
  - The CEO said it hadn't been a priority since 2017, was slowing the company down, and couldn't deliver "a quality of experience of which we can be proud". The community tab now points to Discord. [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/splice-studio-closing)
  - Splice survived on the **sample economy** instead. It has 3M+ samples, acquired Spitfire (April 2025, reported $50M) and Kits AI (January 2026), and launched AI "Variations" in April 2026 that pay sample creators per derived use and per download. [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/splice-launches-ai-tools-that-compensate-sample-creators-when-their-sounds-are-used/)
- **Indaba Music** (2007) ran collaboration, remix contests with Weezer, Peter Gabriel and Linkin Park, and a Creative Commons sample library. Splice acquired it in February 2018. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indaba_Music)
- **Drooble** reached about 1M musicians but stayed niche. It was bootstrapped by a small team for 7+ years and shut down in December 2022 when the founder stepped away. [DMN](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2022/11/29/drooble-is-shutting-down/)
- **Kompoz** is a long-running asynchronous collaboration site with 65k+ collaborations as of 2019.
  - The project owner picks traditional copyright or Creative Commons up front.
  - Finished tracks can be sold, with **revenue splits set in advance and paid out automatically**.
  - Only lossless file upload is behind the paywall. [Reunion Blues, 2019](https://reunionblues.com/blogs/news/how-to-use-kompoz-part-1-2)
- **Vampr** is a networking app for musicians (often called "LinkedIn/Tinder for musicians").
  - It reached 1M users in 2021 and layered on Pro distribution, publishing and marketing. [Mixdown](https://mixdownmag.com.au/news/vampr-hits-1-million-users-networking-app-for-creatives-and-musicians-gears-up-for-q4-funding-round/)
  - The credits database Jaxsta acquired it in 2023, then rebranded as Vinyl Group. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampr)
  - The point: matching alone was not a standalone business.
- **Soundtrap** pitched itself as "the best collaboration platform for making music online". Spotify sold it back to its founders in 2023 while cutting costs. [DMN](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2023/06/12/soundtrap-spotify-sale/)
- **Audiotool** is a free browser DAW with about 2M users (Wikipedia infobox) and Creative Commons remixing. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiotool)
  - Version 3.0 (July 2026) is a rebuilt real-time multiplayer DAW for browsers and tablets, plus an open "NEXUS" SDK with Splice as a launch partner. [AImusicpreneur](https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/audiotool-3-0-multiplayer-daw-nexus-sdk/)
  - How it makes money is not disclosed.

### Duet and layering culture: the collaboration that actually scales
- **Smule** (karaoke duets, where one person records a part and others join it) reported 52M MAU and $101M revenue in 2017. [Wikipedia mirror](https://kiwix.lema.org/wikipedia_en_all_mini_2024-02/A/Smule)
  - No current figures were found **[unverified for 2025]**.
- **Rapchat** claims 5M+ users, 10M+ songs, 50k free beats, collaboration features and a producer earnings portal. [Rapchat](https://rapchat.com/about)
- **TikTok sea shanties (2021).** Strangers used Duet to layer harmonies and instruments onto Nathan Evans's "Wellerman". It led to chart success and a record deal. [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/sea-shanty-tiktok-nathanevanss?lang=en-GB)
- **Ratatouille: The TikTok Musical (2021)** is the clearest multi-role example. Thousands of people contributed songs, dances, makeup, set design and Playbills. A producer later assembled it, credited and paid the original creators, and Disney gave permission. [Scripps/10News](https://www.10news.com/entertainment/ratatouille-the-tiktok-musical-debuts-benefits-out-of-work-actors)
- **Popster** (2023) does mashups of side-by-side videos, AI-generated cover art and royalty-free Mubert beats. It had only a few thousand users, but they averaged about 1.5 hours a day. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/03/popster-music-video-sharing-app-generative-ai-artists)

### Paid-collaboration marketplaces (fees)
- **AirGigs.** $20 to activate; sellers pay 10–15% commission (minimum $8); payouts via PayPal or Payoneer. [AirGigs Help](https://help.airgigs.com/doc/how-much-does-airgigs-charge-me)
- **SoundBetter.** Spotify bought it in 2019 and sold it back to the founders in October 2021. It takes a commission when someone is hired (percentage not stated) and reaches "hundreds of thousands" of musicians. [DMN](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2021/10/11/spotify-sells-soundbetter-to-founders/)
- **Behance** (relevant for cover art):
  - Clients pay 10% + $2 on jobs up to $500, falling to 3% on larger jobs.
  - Freelancers pay 5% down to 0%, plus Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30.
  - If the freelancer has Behance Pro, both sides pay no platform fee. [Behance Help](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/10770324288923-FAQ-What-are-the-fees)
- **Fiverr.** Widely reported at 20% from sellers **[my fetch failed; unverified here]**.
- **Music Video Marketplace** (November 2025) licenses or leases pre-shot footage from filmmakers to artists. [Rolling Stone UK](https://www.rollingstone.co.uk/culture/a-new-concept-for-independent-music-music-video-marketplace-reimagines-visuals-for-artists-55810/)
  - I found no dominant community for music-video creators; Fiverr lists music-video editors as a gig category. [Fiverr](https://block.fiverr.com/hire/music-video-editing)

### SoundCloud: creator tools
- **Plans (December 2024).** "Artist" costs $39 a year and "Artist Pro" $99 a year. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2932941)
- **October 2025 changes.** SoundCloud dropped its cut of distribution revenue (artists now keep 100%) and added a Fan Support button with no commission. It also added vinyl, storefronts and merch. [SoundCloud](https://community.soundcloud.com/playbook-articles/soundcloud-unveils-all-in-one-artist-subscription-more-ways-to-earn-all-in-one-place)
- **AI terms backlash.** In May 2025 users were angered by a February 2024 terms clause allowing AI training. The CEO apologised and promised to use content for voice- or likeness-replicating generative AI only with opt-in consent. [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2025/05/14/soundcloud-update-ai-clause-terms-of-use)
- **Lesson:** the wording of AI rights in a platform's terms is a trust risk in its own right.

### Other tools and communities
- **[untitled]** is a private-sharing and storage app for unreleased tracks: 100K+ MAU, iOS only, $22.6M raised. Users want more collaboration tools. [Daily Dot, May 2025](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/untitled-music-app)
- **Discord** is the default free community layer. A promotional blog lists producer servers of 30k–85k members running challenges, beat battles and feedback **[member counts unverified]**. [BeatSync blog](https://beatsyncpro.ai/blog/music-producer-discord-servers)
- **Musixy.ai** is an AI cover and streaming tool; I found no real collaboration features. [topai.tools](https://topai.tools/t/musixy-ai)

### AI-era co-creation (2025–2026 entrants)
- **Suno** settled with Warner (November 2025).
  - Licensed models replace its current ones. Downloads are limited to paid users, with caps.
  - Artists opt in before their likeness or voice can be used.
  - Suno raised $250M at a $2.45B valuation. [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/11/25/warner-music-inks-suno-deal-amid-cooling-tensions-ai-generated-music/)
- **Udio** settled with UMG (29 October 2025).
  - It **disabled downloads overnight** and kept creations inside a walled garden, which caused user backlash.
  - A licensed remix platform is planned for 2026. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/udio-settles-copyright-lawsuit-partners-with-universal-music-group/)
- **Hook** (AI remix app with social sharing): $10M Series A in February 2026, access to 20M+ licensed songs, label deals, and 45x growth in active users. [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/ai-remix-startup-hook-secures-10m-in-series-a-led-by-khosla-ventures/)
- **GRAI** ("Music with friends", remixes of existing tracks): $9M seed in April 2026. It targets Gen Z and Gen Alpha who want to take part in music "without becoming creators". [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/21/grai-believes-ai-can-make-music-more-social-not-replace-artists/)
- **ElevenMusic** (2026) is a streaming plus licensed-remix platform (Merlin and Kobalt deals). ElevenLabs says it has paid $11M+ to creators through its voice library. [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/elevenlabs-officially-launches-elevenmusic-as-streaming-and-ai-creation-platform-with-licensed-remix-model/)
- **Voice marketplaces:**
  - **Kits Earn** (June 2024): singers make verified voice models and earn on each download. [Record of the Day](https://www.recordoftheday.com/news-and-press/kits-earn-kitsai-invites-vocalists-to-create-an-ai-voice-model-and-unlock-new-revenue)
  - **Voice-Swap**: licensed voices with automatic royalties, BMAT fingerprint checks, and Fairly Trained certification. [DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/free-voice-swap-vst-plugin-will-pay-artists-use-of-ai-vocal-modelling)

### Games
Label-built Roblox experiences became "digital graveyards" because they need live-service upkeep. Fortnite/UEFN concerts drew up to 500k players. Labels are moving toward integrating music into existing platforms rather than building their own. [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/did-these-five-games-and-music-predictions-come-true-in-2025)

### Splits and rights
- **DistroKid Splits**: unlimited collaborators per track; collaborators must create accounts; unclaimed money is held until they join.
  - Splits 2.0 (2021) added recoupment, so people who fronted costs (producer, guest artist, photographer) get paid first. [DistroKid](https://distrokid.com/resources/blog/introducing-splits-automatically-share-earnings), [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/news/industry/distrokid-splits-2-0-recoupment-artist-royalties/)
- **Songtrust**: $100 per writer, then 15% of performance and 20% of mechanical royalties. [Songtrust](https://www.songtrust.com/pricing)
  - Its own guide warns that if a co-writer registers a song first you can be left off.
  - BMI uses a 200% formula versus Songtrust's 100%.
  - Retroactive royalties are typically claimable for about 3 years. [Songtrust](https://www.songtrust.com/all-the-ways-splits-can-go-wrong)
- **Kompoz** is the only collaboration-first platform I found that sets splits at the start of a project and pays them out automatically. [Reunion Blues](https://reunionblues.com/blogs/news/how-to-use-kompoz-part-1-2)

### Moderation, AI and payments
- **Spotify** removed 75M "spammy" tracks in 12 months (September 2025). It allows vocal impersonation only with the artist's authorisation and is pushing a DDEX standard for AI-use credits. [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/09/26/spotify-removes-75-million-spam-tracks-and-introduces-stronger-ai-rules/)
- **Stripe cross-border payouts** only work for **platforms based in the US, UK, EEA, Canada or Switzerland**, paying out within those regions. **A Korea-based platform cannot use them directly.** Global Payouts is limited to US and UK platforms. [Stripe Docs](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/cross-border-payouts)
  - AirGigs-style PayPal and Payoneer payouts are the common workaround. [AirGigs](https://help.airgigs.com/doc/how-much-does-airgigs-charge-me)

## 2. What makes co-creation communities thrive or die

**What kills them:**
- **Collaboration as the whole product.** Endlesss, Blend, Splice Studio and Drooble all died or were cut. Soundtrap and Vampr were sold. The survivors made money elsewhere:
  - BandLab: memberships, paid promotion, distribution.
  - Splice: samples.
  - Smule: subscriptions to a solo karaoke experience that has duets built in.
- **Synchronous real-time jamming.** It is technically impressive but has never sustained a business (Endlesss; Audiotool 3.0 is still unproven).
- **Speculative economics.** Web3's token- and NFT-first designs collapsed when speculation dried up.

**What works:**
- **Asynchronous, low-commitment layering on someone else's seed** (Smule duets, TikTok Duet, BandLab forks). Joining costs a single take.
- **Creation that is useful solo** (BandLab, the Smule solo experience). The community grows out of the output.

**Cold start.** Big platforms seed demand with:
- external virality: TikTok-style sharing out of the app (Hook, Popster),
- contests run with famous artists (Indaba),
- free libraries of material (Audiotool, Rapchat's 50k beats, BandLab Sounds).

**Matching.** Role-based "help wanted" boards (Kompoz) and swipe-style networking (Vampr) produced connections, not finished songs. Vampr reported 7M connections but still got folded into another company.

**Quality.** No platform I found solved quality at beginner level. BandLab's own problem is weak demand from listeners, not a shortage of creators.

**Credit.** Most platforms (BandLab included) leave ownership and splits to off-platform agreements. That gap is real; Kompoz and DistroKid show it can be productised.

## 3. What a beginner-first, multi-role space needs (my synthesis)
1. **A "seed" object** (a hummed melody turned into a MIDI sketch) that others can join one layer at a time, for vocals, cover art or video, the way Smule and TikTok duets work. It should not be a shared DAW.
2. **Typed roles with contribution records attached from the first join** (who did the melody, vocals, video, cover). Store them as structured credits so DDEX-style export works later.
3. **Split agreement at join time** (Kompoz model), defaulting to "no money yet, credit only". Export to DistroKid Splits or Songtrust instead of paying out yourself early.
4. **Licensing chosen at seed creation** (all rights reserved vs Creative Commons-style remix allowed), as Kompoz and Audiotool do.
5. **AI policy written up front:** opt-in only for training and voice cloning. SoundCloud's 2025 backlash shows the cost of vague terms.
6. **Free core plus free sample and beat material** so cost is never the barrier. Monetise only the people who want reach or distribution (the BandLab and SoundCloud pattern).

## 4. Staged path from single-player tool to community
- **Stage 0 – Solo tool.** Hum, then MIDI, then arrange, then export. Make sharing the output to TikTok, Shorts and Reels effortless; that is where demand actually lives (the d4vd lesson).
- **Stage 1 – Remix/fork.** Users can publish a seed with a remix-permission flag, and others fork it (BandLab forks). Count attribution on every fork.
- **Stage 2 – Asynchronous "join" layers.**
  - Vocal take over a seed (Smule).
  - Cover-art layer (uploaded or AI-assisted; Popster did prompt-to-cover).
  - Video layer.
  - Run weekly themed challenges to beat the cold start, using Discord as the first community home (the Splice and producer-server pattern).
- **Stage 3 – Credits and splits.** Structured credit sheets and split agreements, exported to DistroKid or Songtrust.
- **Stage 4 – Paid matching or tips, only once there is real density.** Fan support with no commission (SoundCloud), Behance-style tiered fees, or a Pro plan that waives fees. Avoid holding money yourself until payments are solved.

## 5. Risks
- **Copyright.**
  - Hums of existing songs and covers bring in third-party compositions. You will need fingerprinting or filtering (Voice-Swap uses BMAT) and a DMCA-style takedown flow.
  - AI-generated content without licences triggered lawsuits that forced Suno and Udio into restrictive settlements, including Udio's overnight removal of downloads.
- **Voice and likeness.** Allow impersonation only with authorisation, as Spotify does.
- **Spam and AI flooding.** 75M tracks removed at Spotify shows the scale. A free, AI-assisted platform will attract spam.
- **Co-writer disputes.** Unregistered or conflicting splits cause real lost income (Songtrust). Without a recorded agreement, the platform inherits the dispute.
- **Payments across countries.** Stripe Connect is not self-serve for a Korea-based platform. You would need PayPal or Payoneer, or a US/EU entity. Paying minors and handling KYC across countries add more cost.
- **Durability.** When Endlesss and Blend shut down, users had a few days to export their work. Promising local or exportable project files is itself a trust feature.

---

## Five most decision-relevant findings
1. **Standalone collaboration platforms almost always failed or were sold** (Endlesss, Blend, Splice Studio, Drooble, Soundtrap, Vampr). Survivors monetised a solo tool plus promotion and distribution (BandLab), or content libraries (Splice). Build the single-player tool first.
2. **The collaboration format that scaled is asynchronous "join a seed" layering** (Smule duets, TikTok Duet/sea shanties, Ratatouille's multi-role crowdsourcing), not real-time co-editing.
3. **Credit and split infrastructure is the open gap.** BandLab doesn't arbitrate ownership. Kompoz's pre-agreed automatic splits and DistroKid Splits with recoupment are the models to copy, ideally via export rather than holding money.
4. **AI and copyright policy can decide survival.** Udio cut downloads overnight, Suno now limits downloads to paid users, and SoundCloud took a terms-of-service backlash. Every 2026 AI social entrant (Hook, GRAI, ElevenMusic) leads with label licensing and artist opt-in.
5. **Payments are a structural blocker for a Korea-based global marketplace.** Stripe cross-border Connect works only for platforms in the US, UK, EEA, Canada or Switzerland. Delay paid matching, start with credit-only collaboration and zero-commission tips, and plan a PayPal/Payoneer route or an overseas entity.
