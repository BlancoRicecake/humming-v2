## 1. Competitor profiles

### BandLab: the main competitor
- **What it is and scale:** A free cross-platform DAW plus social network. It has more than 100M registered users (March 2024), up from 60M in January 2023. The US is about 30% of users. Series B1 in 2023 valued it at $425M. https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/music-making-app-bandlab-surpasses-100-million-users/
- **App Store listing:** Says "100M+ creators" and calls it an App of the Year 2025 finalist. https://apps.apple.com/app/968585775
- **Features:** SongStarter (AI song ideas, regenerate three at a time, royalty-free), AutoPitch, Splitter (stem separation), mastering, VoiceTrainer and a social feed. https://apps.apple.com/app/968585775
- **Pricing (official FAQ):**
  - Pro: $14.99/mo, or $99 for the first year then $149/yr.
  - Max: $199 for the first year then $299/yr.
  - Paid perks: AI tools (Voice Cleaner, AutoMix), 32-track projects, distribution, Retune, AI Harmonizer, more sound claims.
  - https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/20758981227033-BandLab-Membership-FAQ
- **Direct threat to HumTrack's core feature:** BandLab now ships Audio-to-MIDI, which uses AI to turn voice, instrument or drum recordings into MIDI on a virtual-instrument track. It works on web and mobile and is **Membership-only**. https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/37575596722329-Converting-Audio-to-MIDI
- **More Membership-only AI tools:** Layer adds chords, bass and drums around existing MIDI. Extend and Recompose are also included. This is close to HumTrack's hum → MIDI → auto-backing flow. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/bandlab-guides/bandlab-smart-tools-midi-ai-guide (third-party guide, Aug/Sep 2026)
- **Export is free in the official docs:**
  - Web: tracks as MIDI or WAV, mixdowns as M4A or 16-bit WAV.
  - iOS: tracks as MIDI, WAV or M4A.
  - Android: mixdown only (M4A or MP4).
  - No membership requirement is stated. https://help.bandlab.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036010533-Supported-Import-and-Export-File-Formats
- **Monetization squeeze and backlash, 2026:**
  - v11.23.0 (May 2026) moved sound packs behind the paywall, limited free users to 20 samples a month and added aggressive ads.
  - The average rating for that version fell from 4.19 to 2.76.
  - About 694K US iOS downloads in 30 days.
  - https://mwm.ai/articles/app-updates/bandlab-v11-23-0-paywalls-beat-samples-and-adds-aggressive-ads-may-2026
- **Japan paywall test (key for HumTrack):**
  - BandLab support told a Japanese user it is trialling a rule in Japan that **requires Membership to open Studio at all**, with a 3-day free trial.
  - Japanese reviewers are angry, and one said they would switch software (review dated 9月7日; year not shown, likely 2026).
  - JP pricing: Pro ¥2,500/mo or ¥25,000/yr; Max ¥50,000/yr.
  - The JP store title leads with AI: "AIと一緒に、音楽制作アプリ" ("a music-making app, together with AI").
  - https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/bandlab-ai%E3%81%A8%E4%B8%80%E7%B7%92%E3%81%AB-%E9%9F%B3%E6%A5%BD%E5%88%B6%E4%BD%9C%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA/id968585775

### GarageBand (iOS and Mac only)
- Free, rated 4.1 from about 118K ratings. Features include Live Loops, Drummer and a free Sound Library. The listing mentions no AI features. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garageband/id408709785
- Export is free because the app is free. There is no Android or Windows version (general knowledge; not re-checked this session).
- Weakness for HumTrack's audience: no hum-to-MIDI and no auto-arrangement.

### Soundtrap
- Spotify sold it back to its founders in 2023. Spotify had bought it in 2017 for about $30M. https://routenote.com/blog/spotify-sells-soundtrap/
- Now independent and based in Stockholm. Its focus is split across Music Makers, Podcasters and Education. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soundtrap
- **[UNVERIFIED]** I could not confirm a Noteflight merger or acquisition. The Soundtrap and Noteflight Wikipedia pages mention none.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A third-party listing gives Free, Basic $5/mo and Pro $15/mo. The official pricing page did not render. https://toolradar.com/tools/soundtrap/pricing

### FL Studio Mobile
- $14.99 upfront on iOS, plus add-on packs at $0.99–$3.99 (checked 2026-03-21). https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/432850619
- Pay once, export included. It is a full DAW, so the learning curve is steep.

### Koala Sampler
- $4.99 upfront on iOS. In-app purchases: Samurai Edition $3.99, Mixer $4.99, sample packs $2.99 (checked 2026-08-30). https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1449584007
- Built for beatmakers and sampling, not for melody-first creators.

### Ableton Note
- $6.99 upfront on iOS, plus a $3.99 Drift in-app purchase. https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1633243177
- An idea sketchpad that feeds into Ableton Live. Its users are aspiring producers.

### Groovepad (Easybrain)
- Rated 4.8 from about 518K ratings on iOS. It is a loop and pad toy with "Beat School" lessons. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1454398991
- Subscription: $7.99/week, $9.99–14.99/mo, $46.99/yr.
- About 200K+ monthly downloads and about $60K/mo revenue (estimate captured April 2024). https://adapty.io/paywall-library/groovepad/
- Weakness: ads and pack gating, and you cannot create an original melody.

### Medly
- Rated 4.6 from 38K ratings. Membership is $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr with a 7-day trial. https://apps.apple.com/app/medly/id940268124
- **Free tier:** 17 instruments, 100+ loops, and audio recording capped at 4 bars (members get 30 seconds).
- Shares as audio, video and MIDI. **[UNVERIFIED]** whether sharing or export is gated.
- Its section-based arranging is structurally the closest UI to HumTrack.

### Splice Mobile
- A companion app to the sample subscription. Create mode builds loop "Stacks" and exports to Ableton or Studio One, or as a stereo bounce. Rated 4.6 from 666 ratings. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splice-make-music-now/id1108532275
- Bought Spitfire Audio in April 2025 (about $50M per the Financial Times). https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/04/28/spitfire-audio-splice-acquisition/
- Its users are producers, not beginners.

### Audiotool
- 3.0 launched on 2026-07-09: a free browser DAW, rebuilt with real-time multiplayer.
- NEXUS is an open SDK. It connects to your own LLM via MCP. Launch partners include Splice and UJAM.
- Native mobile apps are "on the way." https://mixdownmag.com.au/news/audiotool-3-0-launches-with-multiplayer-daw-and-open-sdk/

### Voloco (vocal-first)
- "50 MILLION DOWNLOADS." Features: auto-tune, beat library, and WAV/AAC vocal export. https://applevis.com/apps/ios/music/voloco-vocal-recording-studio
- All Access: $11.99/mo or $49.99–59.99/yr. https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1052970183

### Rapchat
- 7M users in 2021, when it raised $2.3M. https://techcrunch.com/2021/04/30/rapchat-tunes-into-2-3m-as-its-music-making-app-hits-7m-users/
- Gold and Platinum in-app purchases range from $7.99 to $49.99 (April 2026). https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/804913240

### Smule (karaoke and social)
- Rated 4.4 from about 780K ratings across versions. Subscriptions are weekly, monthly or yearly. https://mixrank.com/appstore/apps/509993510
- **[UNVERIFIED]** I found no current user count.

### Japan-native social comparable: nana
- Overdub and duet singing app. It had 3M users in 2017, about 60% of them Japanese. https://www.musicconnection.com/nana-music-creators-worldwide/ (old data)
- Relevant to HumTrack's Japanese TikTok traction and its community vision.

### Music Maker JAM (Magix)

### Hum-to-song apps (closest to HumTrack's core)
- **ByteDance Ripple (2023):**
  - You sing or hum, and "Melody to Song" generates drums, bass and keys in a chosen genre, with DAW-style editing.
  - It launched as an iOS-only, invite-only US beta.
  - Sources: https://musically.com/2023/06/30/bytedance-reveals-ai-powered-music-creation-app-ripple/ and https://www.musicradar.com/news/bytedance-ripple-music-making-app
  - **[UNVERIFIED]** Its 2025–26 status. I found no evidence it scaled.
- **HumToSong (iOS, Nov 2025, solo developer):** Fewer than 1K downloads, rated 2.6. https://mwm.ai/apps/humtosong/6755473455
- **Humming to Music Maker (Android, EveryBit):** 5K+ downloads, rated about 3.1, ads. Lets you pick from 8 instruments and view or copy the notes. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hummingmusic.maker&hl=en_US
- **Takeaway:** This niche is real but has only poor-quality apps so far. No well-funded consumer hum-to-melody DAW has scaled.

### AI generators competing for the same "I want to make a song" intent
- **Suno:**
  - Claims 100M users, about 2M paid subscribers and about $300M ARR (Feb 2026), at a $5.4B valuation. https://eesel.ai/blog/suno-review

  - An AI DAW where you can start from a vocal idea, riff or MIDI. Aimed at beginners and pros.

## 3. Gaps HumTrack could own

1. **A good-quality, mobile-native "hum → editable MIDI → backing → arrange" flow for beginners.**
   - Ripple tried it and stalled [UNVERIFIED].
   - The current hum-to-song apps are rated around 2.6–3.1 with under 5K downloads.
   - BandLab has the parts (Audio-to-MIDI plus Layer), but they sit behind a $14.99/mo paywall inside a complex DAW.
2. **Price.**
   - HumTrack's $3.49/mo is about a quarter of BandLab Pro, a third of Medly or Voloco, and a third of Suno Pro.
   - It is also cheaper than Groovepad's weekly plan.
3. **"You made it" ownership versus AI generators.**
   - Suno and Udio are tightening rights and downloads: free users get no commercial use and 7 lifetime downloads, Udio is moving to no downloads, and Suno says users are generally not the owner.
   - HumTrack's output starts from the user's own melody. That is a clear message: your melody, your song.
4. **Japan specifically.** BandLab is angering Japanese users by trialling a Studio paywall. That opens a window for a cheap, Japanese-friendly beginner app.
5. **Android plus desktop parity.**
   - GarageBand, Ableton Note, Medly and ElevenMusic are iOS-only.
   - BandLab on Android exports only the mixdown, with no per-track or MIDI export.
6. **Community for producers, singers, music-video makers and cover-art designers.** BandLab owns the generic version. A niche, melody-first collaboration layer is still open, but see the Endlesss warning in Threats.

## 4. Threats

- **BandLab** already has free tools, 100M+ users, Audio-to-MIDI, Layer, SongStarter and social features. If it makes Audio-to-MIDI free, or bundles it into SongStarter, HumTrack's main differentiator shrinks.
- **Suno, Gemini, ElevenMusic and CapCut** satisfy the "I just want a song" intent instantly and for free. Gemini and CapCut are free at massive scale. If Suno's audio input or Gemini adds humming input for free, the casual segment erodes.
- **Mozart AI** is funded, has a mobile app, starts from vocal ideas and targets beginners. It is the closest new entrant to watch.
- **Community-first music apps have a poor survival record:** Endlesss closed in 2024. Smule and nana show that a social layer needs a singing or performance hook, not production alone.

## 5. Pricing benchmarks: is export free?

- **Export is free on almost every DAW-style competitor:**
  - BandLab: free WAV/MIDI/M4A export per the official help docs.
  - GarageBand: free app, free export.
  - FL Studio Mobile, Koala and Ableton Note: paid once, export included.
  - Voloco: free beats and WAV vocal export per its listing.
- **Where export is restricted:**
  - AI generators: Suno free gets 7 lifetime downloads and no commercial rights. Udio is moving to no downloads.
  - Loop toys like Groovepad gate sound packs and features.
- **Where competitors put the paywall instead:**
  - BandLab: AI and premium tools, sample allowance, track count, distribution and mastering.
  - Medly: instrument count and recording length (4 bars free).
  - Groovepad: sound packs.
- **Typical paid ranges:**
  - Beginner subscriptions run $9.99–14.99/mo and $46.99–149/yr.
  - Paid-upfront DAWs cost $4.99–14.99 one-time.
  - HumTrack, at $3.49/mo and $33.49/yr, is the cheapest subscription in this set.

## Five findings that matter most for decisions

1. **HumTrack's export paywall is out of step with DAW competitors.** BandLab, GarageBand and the paid-upfront apps all let users export. Only AI generators like Suno cap downloads, and they are criticised for it. Consider allowing a limited free export, such as a watermarked or low-quality MP3, or a few exports a month. Gate quality (WAV/stems/MIDI), count or AI features instead.
2. **BandLab already offers hum/voice → MIDI (Audio-to-MIDI) and AI backing (Layer), both Membership-only at $14.99/mo.** HumTrack's edge is a simpler, mobile-first flow at about a quarter of the price, not the feature itself.
3. **BandLab is trialling "Membership required to open Studio" in Japan, and Japanese users are pushing back.** This lines up with HumTrack's Japanese TikTok traction and is a time-limited opening for Japan-focused acquisition.
4. **Dedicated hum-to-song consumer apps are weak or stalled.** Examples are ByteDance Ripple (2023 beta; current status unverified), HumToSong (fewer than 1K downloads) and EveryBit (5K downloads). There is no scaled product in the exact niche.
5. **AI generators are restricting ownership and downloads.** Suno free gets 7 lifetime personal-use downloads, users are generally not the owner, and Udio is a walled garden. Meanwhile Gemini and CapCut offer free text-to-music. Position HumTrack as "your melody, your song, you own it" rather than competing on instant generation. Watch Mozart AI, the funded beginner AI DAW with a mobile app, as the nearest new entrant.
